Frequently asked questions about genuine leather

Do LEA codes identify cowhide or another leather type?

The catalogue currently identifies only the genuine leather category and LEA sample codes. Species, layer, origin and tanning method have not been confirmed for individual codes; request documentation before ordering.

Can full-grain or top-grain leather be identified from photos?

Do not use catalogue photographs to confirm grade or finish. Request the technical description for the LEA code and check a physical sample.

What should I check when approving a genuine leather sample?

Check the surface, colour under actual-use lighting, thickness and tolerances, dimensions or usable area, and appearance acceptance criteria. Agree these values for the batch and intended use.

Will the delivered sample match the colour in the photo?

Displayed colours depend on the screen and photography lighting. Approve a physical sample, confirm the colour code and check the batch before volume production.

How can I get advice and a quotation?

Send the sample code, application, estimated quantity and technical requirements through the contact form or Zalo at 0931 792 811. Pricing, physical samples and availability must be confirmed directly.
